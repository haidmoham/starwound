# Prior art and research leads

These are references, not vendored implementations. Verify licenses, model assumptions, and maintenance before reuse. Keep attribution alongside any adapted code. No commercial recording or film asset is included.

## Physics / Interstellar direction

- [REBOUND](https://rebound.hanno-rein.de/): reference implementations and documentation for orbital experiments and numerical integration. Useful as an independent model-validation laboratory; not a reason to add a Python service to this browser app.
- [Eric Bruneton: black-hole shader](https://ebruneton.github.io/black_hole_shader/): a specific non-rotating-black-hole beam-tracing renderer. A later observer/lensing research branch, not a drop-in label for the current Newtonian world.
- [Interstellar / DNGR paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/1502.03808): light propagation and rendering around a spinning black hole; distinguish physical modeling from deliberate cinematic choices.
- [Falstad ripple tank](https://www.falstad.com/ripple/): audition driven waves, interference, boundaries, and resonance before committing to a solver.
- [VisualPDE](https://visualpde.com/): explore scientific field models and boundary conditions.

## Biology / life direction

- [Amanda Ghassaei: gpu-io examples](https://apps.amandaghassaei.com/gpu-io/examples/) and [source](https://github.com/amandaghassaei/gpu-io): agent/field and reaction-diffusion starting points. Prefer causal coupling through shared state over blending unrelated pictures.
- [Karl Sims: reaction-diffusion](https://www.karlsims.com/rd.html): parameter regions, feed/removal, diffusion, and spatial variation.
- [Andy Lomas: Cellular Forms](https://andylomas.com/cellularForms.html): local growth rules and emergent form.
- [Flow Lenia](https://github.com/erwanplantec/FlowLenia): a later artificial-life branch, not required infrastructure for the first piece.
- [Growing Neural Cellular Automata](https://distill.pub/2020/growing-ca/): a later route to identity, growth, and repair. Arbitrary audio conditioning would require its own training/robustness experiments.

## Composition and exploration

- [Max Cooper: Emergence](https://maxcooper.net/emergence): science, music, and visual work can remain coherent across different model families.
- [Andy Lomas: Species Explorer](https://andylomas.com/extra/andylomas_species_explorer_eva2016.pdf): inspiration for selecting and branching among candidate parameter configurations. Start with saved auditions, not a machine-learning search pipeline.
- *Nabokov* by Fontaines D.C. and *Interstellar* are artistic influences. Use a local copy supplied by the artist for recording-specific development, and generated fixtures for automated tests.
